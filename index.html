Crawlspace

Five floors down. One way out.

How to Play

Tap a topic to open it.

The goal

Fight your way down five floors. Every floor is a new random maze. The stairs down sit at the far end of each floor, and an enemy always guards them. On floor 5 the stairs are replaced by the exit, guarded by the Ogre King. Beat him and step through to win.

You have one life. If your health hits 0, the run ends and you see a summary of how far you got.

Moving and the map

Tap the side of the map you want to step toward: top, bottom, left or right of the middle. You only see the tiles right around you and the path you have already walked. Everything else stays dark, including chests, shops and enemies, so explore to find them.

  • You (the small blue square)
  • Chest: coins, a potion, a piece of gear or a scroll. It turns dark once opened.
  • Stairs down. Step on them to go to the next floor.
  • Merchant: floor 2 (Iron gear) and floor 4 (Steel gear)
  • Enchanter: floor 3 only
  • The exit: floor 5

An enemy you have met stays marked on the map in its color. A white outline marks the Ogre King.

Fighting

Walk into an enemy to start a fight. The view switches to the combat screen: you stand on the left, and the enemies stand in a staggered line on the right, each drawn as its own creature with a green health bar above it. Enemies can come in groups of up to 3 on deeper floors. A * after a name means that enemy carries gear, so it hits harder or has armor.

Tap an enemy to target it. A yellow ring under an enemy shows your target, and your attacks go to it. When your target falls, the next enemy is targeted for you.

Damage rises over the one who took it as a red number, healing as a green one. MISS appears over whoever an attack was aimed at, STUNNED over a stunned enemy, and SECOND WIND over you when that perk saves your life.

You first choose Fight or Flee. Fleeing works 25% of the time and puts you back one step. If it fails, the enemies get a free attack. You can never flee the Ogre King.

Each turn of a fight, pick one move. The enemies then take their turn.

  • Normal: your reliable hit, 8-14 damage plus your weapon and bonuses, minus the enemy's armor. It misses 10% of the time, and 10% of hits are critical hits for double damage.
  • Heavy: 1.75 times the damage, but it misses 45% of the time, and enemies hit you 20% harder that turn. Use it to finish a fight faster when you can afford the risk.
  • Block: you don't attack, but you take only a quarter of the damage, and each hit on your shield has a 10% chance to stun that enemy so it skips its next attack.

Enemies miss 10% of the time too, and 10% of their hits are critical hits for 1.5 times the damage. Your armor takes points off every hit, but a hit always does at least 1.

When a fight ends you get coins and XP for each enemy, and the group drops a piece of gear. You can Equip it or Leave it, and either one takes you back to the map.

Enemies and their tricks

Every enemy has a special ability. The message log tells you when it happens.

Floors 1-2

  • Bat: hard to hit. You miss it 30% of the time instead of 10%, so Heavy is a poor choice.
  • Slime: when killed it splits into two Little Slimes (weak, but they still hit you).
  • Goblin: each hit has a 33% chance to steal 3-6 of your coins (once). Three turns later it runs off with them. Kill it first to get your coins back.
  • Skeleton (floor 2 on): the first time it dies it may pull itself back together (33%) with a quarter of its health, unless the killing blow was a critical hit.

Floors 3-4

  • Orc: bloodlust. Hits 4 harder while you are below half health, so heal before it gets going.
  • Troll: regenerates 6 health every turn. A critical hit stops it for one turn, and fire stops it while the Troll burns. Finish Trolls fast.
  • Dark Knight: often well armored. Whenever you miss it, it counterattacks for free, so a Heavy miss is costly.
  • Wraith: heals itself for a quarter of the damage it deals to you.

Floors 4-5

  • Minotaur: its first attack in a fight is a charge that never misses and deals double damage. Block it.
  • Wyvern: each hit has a 35% chance to poison you: 3 damage every turn and every step for 4 turns. A potion cures poison. Your health bar turns purple while you are poisoned.

The Ogre King

260 health and armor of his own. Every third attack is a Crushing Blow that ignores your armor. The turn before, the log warns you: "The Ogre King winds up a huge swing..." That is your cue to Block or drink a potion. Below half health he flies into a rage and hits 3 harder.

Health and potions

You start with 100 health, and it does not come back on its own. Your ways to heal:

  • Potions: heal 45 and cure poison. Tap Potion while exploring or during a fight (drinking in a fight uses your turn).
  • Level-ups: heal 20.
  • Some perks and enchantments, like Vitality, Mending and Vampiric.

You can carry 5 potions. The Potion Belt (40 coins, both merchants) raises that to 7, and the Potion Bandolier (70 coins, floor 4, needs the Belt) to 10. Each merchant has 5 potions for sale at 10 coins. If a chest holds a potion while your bag is full, you get coins instead.

Levels and perk cards

Kills give XP (bigger enemies give more), shown by the thin gold bar under your health. The first level needs 25 XP, and each level after needs 20 more than the last.

Each level-up heals 20 and deals you three perk cards. Pick one. A card shows the total you would have after taking it, and its edge shows how rare it is: grey is common, blue uncommon, gold rare. One card is always a basic. Don't like the hand? Redraw costs 10 coins, then 20, then 30 in the same level-up.

Every perk can be taken again and again. The specials grow a little less with each repeat, and specials you already own turn up more often.

Basics

  • Attack: +2 damage on every hit.
  • Vitality: +25 max health (and heals 25), and Block lets 15% less damage through.
  • Focus: +3% critical hit chance, and every miss chance shrinks by 8% (including Heavy and against Bats).

Specials

  • Pathfinder: see 1 more tile around you, and enemies within sight show on the map before you meet them. Your first hit on an enemy you spotted first is an Ambush for +50% damage.
  • Executioner: +50% damage to enemies below half health.
  • Antidote: potions heal 10 more, and poison is weaker and shorter.
  • Shield Bash: when you Block, you also bash your target for 4 damage, and the stun chance rises to 20%.
  • Second Wind (rare): the next blow that would kill you leaves you at 1 health instead. Used up when it saves you; each pick gives one save.
  • Tight Purse: Goblins can't steal from you, and you find 30% more gold.
Gear and armor

You have five slots: weapon, helmet, chest, bracers and greaves. Gear comes in three materials:

  • Basic: Rusty Sword, Leather Cap, Leather Vest, Cloth Wraps, Leather Boots
  • Iron: Iron Sword, Iron Helm, Chainmail, Iron Bracers, Iron Greaves
  • Steel: Steel Sword, Steel Helm, Steel Plate, Steel Bracers, Steel Greaves

A weapon adds its number to your damage. Armor numbers add up to your Defense, which comes off every enemy hit. Full Steel armor gives 13. Gear drops from fights and chests, and merchants sell it (price = 5 coins per point). Better material also lets you use stronger enchantments.

Everything you wear shows on your character in fights, from a rusty sword to a steel great helm. Wear all four armor pieces (helmet, chest, bracers and greaves) and you become a knight, with a blue cape and a tabard bearing a gold cross; with a steel helm you also get a plume. A full set of Steel adds gold trim, and enchanted gear glows in its enchantment's color.

Enchantments and scrolls

Any piece of gear can hold one enchantment. It stays on that item, so if you swap the item for another, its enchantment goes with it. The same enchantment on several items stacks.

Enchantments come in three tiers. Minor fits any gear, Good needs Iron or Steel gear, and Major needs Steel gear. Numbers below are Minor / Good / Major.

Weapon

  • Flaming: hits set the enemy on fire for 2 / 3 / 4 damage a turn for 3 turns. A burning Troll can't regenerate.
  • Vampiric: heal 5% / 8% / 12% of the damage you deal.
  • Balanced: Heavy misses 15% / 25% / 35% less often.
  • Giantslayer: +10% / 20% / 30% damage to big enemies (100+ health).
  • Swift: 8% / 12% / 16% chance to strike a second time.

Armor

  • Thorns: enemies that hit you take 2 / 3 / 5 damage.
  • Mending: heal 3 / 5 / 8 after every fight you win.
  • Fortitude: +10 / 20 / 30 max health.
  • Purity: poison lasts 1 / 2 / 3 fewer turns, and Wraith drain is cut 25% / 50% / 75%.
  • Fleetfoot: +10% / 15% / 20% chance to flee.
  • Fortune: +10% / 20% / 30% gold.

Where to get them

  • Enchanted drops: 1 in 5 pieces of gear comes enchanted. Good ones show up from floor 2, Major from floor 4.
  • Scrolls: found in chests. Put it on a piece of gear right away, keep it, or throw it away. Your bag holds 2 scrolls (3 with the Scroll Satchel, 25 coins at the merchants). Tap Scrolls to use a kept one later, for example once you find Iron gear for a Good scroll.
  • The Enchanter (floor 3): pick a piece of gear, an enchantment and a tier. Minor costs 15 coins, Good 30, Major 50.

Putting a new enchantment on an item replaces the old one. The game warns you first.

Gold and shops

Gold comes from kills and chests, and there is never enough of it. Spend it at the merchants on floors 2 and 4 (potions, potion bags, the Scroll Satchel, gear), at the Enchanter on floor 3, and on Redraws. The floor 4 merchant is the last shop, so after that gold is only good for Redraws. Spend it while you can.

Tips
  • Clear each floor before taking the stairs. Every fight is XP, gold and a chance at gear.
  • Drink potions between fights, not halfway through a dangerous one, unless the next hit could kill you.
  • Block the Ogre King's Crushing Blow and the Minotaur's opening charge.
  • Kill Trolls fast, and kill Goblins before they run off with your gold.
  • Skip Heavy against Bats and Dark Knights, where misses hurt most.
  • Save a potion or two for the Ogre King.
  • The side panel under the map shows your gear, defense, level, gold, potions, scrolls and perks.
